ScamShield AI generates probabilistic mathematical predictions based on trained text representations and passive lexical heuristics. A score of 0 does not constitute an absolute guarantee of safety, and a score of 100 does not constitute a formal legal declaration of criminality.

Users must exercise critical judgment before sharing banking credentials, passwords, or executing financial transactions. ScamShield AI is an advisory aid and not a substitute for institutional verification through official customer support channels.
